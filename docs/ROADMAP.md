# Roadmap

## Stage 1 — content line

- research backlog from text ideas and account/source notes;
- planning horizon from one to five weeks;
- human edit, request-changes and approval states;
- five editable platform drafts;
- local static visual using local photo and brand tokens;
- publication-disabled local package.

## Stage 2 — local 3D profile

- consent and likeness acceptance;
- local reference set;
- geometry, material and background-compositing checks;
- no video requirement at this stage.

## Stage 3 — 3D video

- speech and movement;
- authenticated provider adapter on the operator's machine;
- cost and generation receipts;
- separate approval for every exported clip.

Presentation integration remains a secondary handoff and does not block these stages.
